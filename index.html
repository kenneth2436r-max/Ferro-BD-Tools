Commission & GST Settings

Set these once - they become the default commission % pre-filled into every weekly/monthly platform table. Existing numbers you've already typed into a table are never overwritten automatically; these are just the starting defaults for new entries.

Platform commission rates

Base % is the platform's actual service fee. Most platforms also charge GST on top of their own fee (a separate charge from the booking's GST) - Effective % is what actually gets deducted from gross revenue, and is what the tables use.

PlatformBase Commission %GST Applies to This Fee?Effective Commission % (used in tables)
Example: District's base fee is 7%. GST at 18% applies to that fee, so the effective deduction is 7% x 1.18 = 8.26% - that's the "7% + GST" language you'll see in platform notices.

How this works

Weekly reports (every Tuesday) are about pace and pipeline: how much came in this week, whether any promo affected the numbers, what leads moved, and what went wrong. They don't need month-over-month graphs - just fill in the platform table, note leads and problems, and generate the text to send.

Monthly reports (first Tuesday of the month) are the full picture: bookings, coaching, footfall, notable events, and - the part weekly reports don't need - comparison to previous months with graphs, so you and your manager can see the trend, not just one month in isolation.

The math, once, so it's never inconsistent again: for every platform, Commission = Gross Revenue x Commission%. After-commission amount = Gross - Commission. That amount still has GST folded into it (customers already paid GST-inclusive prices), so: GST = After-commission x 18/118, and Net Revenue = After-commission - GST. That Net Revenue figure - never the gross figure - is what belongs in a report as "Bookings/Events Revenue." This tool always does it that way automatically.

Where your data lives: everything you type is saved automatically in this browser (localStorage), so closing and reopening this file keeps your history. That said, browser storage can be wiped by clearing browsing data, and it won't follow you to a different computer - use Export Backup (JSON) after saving a month, and keep those files somewhere safe (e.g. a "Reports" folder in your Drive). Import Backup loads them back in if you ever need to.

Printing: the Print/Save as PDF button uses your browser's print dialog - choose "Save as PDF" as the destination if you want a PDF file instead of a physical printout.
